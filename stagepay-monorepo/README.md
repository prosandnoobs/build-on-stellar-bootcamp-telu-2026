🎸 THE STAGEPAY PLATFORM 🎸
⚡ PROJECT TITLE: STAGEPAY DECENTRALIZED TICKETING
🕵️‍♂️ PROJECT DESCRIPTION
StagePay is a specialized music event management and ticket sales platform built on the Stellar blockchain. It provides a permanent, immutable, and cryptographically secure record for concert access. Whether it's a VVIP backstage pass or a general festival admission, StagePay ensures every purchase is verified on-chain and settled instantly in XLM. Stop relying on easily forged paper stubs; start relying on the ledger.

🔭 PROJECT VISION
"Transparency in every transaction."

We envision a live music industry where every "I bought a ticket" is backed by the unforgeable security of a global blockchain. Our goal is to:

Codify Concert Access: Turning ticket tiers and venue entries into structured, verifiable data.

Eradicate Scalping: Organizing the chaos of secondary markets through transparent, immutable ownership.

Cryptographic Proof: Ensuring that once a ticket is purchased, your spot in the crowd stands as a historical fact.

🛠 KEY FEATURES
[ON-CHAIN TICKETING] - Every ticket purchased is a permanent part of the Stellar ledger, tied directly to the buyer's wallet.

[XLM SETTLEMENT] - Seamless, low-fee payments using native Stellar lumens with real-time IDR conversion.

[TIERED INVENTORY] - Smart filtering for dynamic pricing across VVIP, VIP, Tribun, and Festival categories.

[QR LEDGER PROOF] - Automatic generation of cryptographic QR codes linked directly to transaction hashes and memos.
